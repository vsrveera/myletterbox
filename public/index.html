Life Cabinet

AI-powered · Personal document vault

Every important document in your life, organized automatically.

Life Cabinet reads, classifies, and files your letters, bills, and contracts the moment they arrive — organized around the people, property, and accounts they actually belong to, not folders.

Sign in with the Google account you use to send letters to your AgentMail inbox.

Why Life Cabinet

🗂️

Organized by life, not folders

Documents attach to real things — your car, your apartment, your insurance policy — so everything about them lives in one place.

✨

AI reads and files for you

Every document is automatically classified, tagged, and summarized — no manual sorting required.

⏰

Never miss a deadline

Renewals, expirations, and bills due are surfaced on your dashboard before they become a problem.

🔎

Find anything in seconds

Search by keyword, category, asset, or date — and jump straight to the document you need.
